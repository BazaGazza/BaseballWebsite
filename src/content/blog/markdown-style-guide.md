Intro:
Our project will be a locally hosted web application that uses a portable SQLite database stored with the project repository. The application will analyze how ballpark, opponent, scheduling, and weather conditions influence MLB attendance and scoring. Users will be able to explore league-wide trends through an overview dashboard, investigate individual stadiums, and compare specific matchups using filters such as home team, visiting team, stadium, day of the week, game time, weather, and season range. The project will demonstrate database concepts including normalization, foreign keys, views, joins, aggregate queries, indexes, and query-performance analysis while remaining simple for all team members to download, run, and present.
Although the initial application will focus on historical MLB data, the concept could have broader commercial potential. 

A professional version could be licensed or sold to individual teams as an analytics tool for identifying high-demand matchups, planning promotions, and supporting ticket-pricing decisions. The application could also generate revenue through targeted advertising, paid access to advanced analytics, or subscription-based reports and APIs. Because the database structure and queries could be adapted to other sports, the same concept could eventually analyze attendance and venue conditions for basketball, football, hockey, soccer, or other sporting events.
